The Adventure

For twenty years, rains of asteroids have fallen on the Earth. Win the levels of the map one after the other, each one a rain of its own, up to the Colossus at the end of the first world.

Good to know

  • Each level has three stars: win it, save more than half of your inhabitants, and meet its challenge, told on the map.
  • Your stars buy bonuses kept from a level to the next, in the map's Upgrades. Take them back to spend them again.
  • Each level's pause menu sells a few upgrades, to learn them one at a time.
  • A level lost, or won without all its stars: the NAZA gives you a tip.
  • Easy, Normal or Hard: each difficulty is an adventure of its own, with its own stars.

Your mission

Asteroids rain on the Earth from every direction. Your weapons fire on their own: you steer the Gravitron, gather the debris it pulls in as mat, and spend your mat on upgrades in the pause menu.

Good to know

  • The open part of the Gravitron pulls the debris in… and the asteroids too.
  • An asteroid crashing on the Earth takes away as many million inhabitants as it has life left.
  • A new threat every two minutes, announced by the NAZA: comets, splitting rocks, giants after six minutes (destroy them first), swarms, armored rocks.
  • The research tree is open: build a Research Lab with your planet's upgrades, then start a research from the pause menu. It takes time, not mat.
